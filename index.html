Rohingya Youth Development Society Network
(RYDSN)

A youth-led organization committed to empowering Rohingya young people and communities through education, skills development, leadership, protection, advocacy, and community-based initiatives.

Our goal is to support young people to become confident, skilled, responsible, and active contributors to their communities and to create opportunities for a safer, more inclusive, and hopeful future.

Learn About RYDSN Support Our Work

1. Introduction & About Us

Rohingya Youth Development Society Network (RYDSN) is a community-focused youth network working to strengthen the capacity, participation, and leadership of Rohingya youth. RYDSN believes that young people are an important source of positive change.

Who We Are

RYDSN is a youth-focused community network dedicated to strengthening the capacity and participation of Rohingya youth.

What We Do

We work across areas including education, youth leadership, skills development, protection awareness, advocacy, community engagement, and social inclusion.

What We Believe

We believe that every young person deserves the opportunity to learn, develop their potential, participate meaningfully, and contribute to a better future.

2. Our Mission

“To empower Rohingya youth through education, skills development, leadership, protection, advocacy, and meaningful community participation.”

RYDSN works to create opportunities for young people to strengthen their capacities, express their voices, develop leadership skills, and contribute positively to their communities.

3. Our Vision

“A safe, empowered, educated, and resilient Rohingya community where every young person has the opportunity to learn, lead, participate, and build a better future.”

RYDSN envisions a future where Rohingya youth are recognized as valuable members of society and are able to participate meaningfully in decisions that affect their lives.

4. Our Key Objectives

What RYDSN aims to achieve in our community

Promote education and lifelong learning opportunities for youth.
Develop leadership and life skills among young people.
Increase youth participation in community decision-making.
Promote protection, dignity, inclusion, and equal opportunities.
Support youth-led community initiatives.
Strengthen awareness of rights and responsibilities.
Encourage peaceful coexistence and community solidarity.
Promote skills development and livelihood-related opportunities.
Build partnerships with local, national, and international organizations.
Create platforms where Rohingya youth can share their ideas, concerns, and solutions.

5. Our Core Values

Guiding principles behind everything we do

Inclusion

Everyone deserves to be respected and included regardless of background, gender, age, ability, or social status.

Youth Leadership

Young people should have meaningful opportunities to lead, participate, and contribute to decisions affecting their communities.

Dignity

We respect the dignity, rights, and voices of every individual.

Accountability

We are committed to responsible, transparent, and ethical work.

Equality

We promote equal opportunities and fair participation for all.

Respect

We value diversity, dialogue, cooperation, and mutual respect.

Community Ownership

We encourage communities and young people to take an active role in identifying challenges and developing solutions.
