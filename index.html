1. Introduction & 15. About Us

Who We Are & What We Believe

Rohingya Youth Development Society Network (RYDSN) is a community-focused youth network working to strengthen the capacity, participation, and leadership of Rohingya youth.

RYDSN believes that young people are an important source of positive change. Through meaningful youth participation, education, training, awareness activities, community engagement, and advocacy, RYDSN works to help young people develop the knowledge and skills they need to contribute to their communities.

The organization seeks to connect youth, community members, local organizations, humanitarian actors, and other stakeholders to promote sustainable and inclusive development.

"We believe that every young person deserves the opportunity to learn, develop their potential, participate meaningfully, and contribute to a better future."

Core Summary

Who We Are

A youth-focused community network dedicated to strengthening the capacity and participation of Rohingya youth.

What We Do

We work across areas including education, youth leadership, skills development, protection awareness, advocacy, community engagement, and social inclusion.

Our Primary Goal

To support young people to become confident, skilled, responsible, and active contributors to their communities and create opportunities for a safer, more inclusive, and hopeful future.

2. Our Vision

Our Vision Statement

“A safe, empowered, educated, and resilient Rohingya community where every young person has the opportunity to learn, lead, participate, and build a better future.”

RYDSN envisions a future where Rohingya youth are recognized as valuable members of society and are able to participate meaningfully in decisions that affect their lives and communities.

3. Our Mission

Our Mission Statement

“To empower Rohingya youth through education, skills development, leadership, protection, advocacy, and meaningful community participation.”

RYDSN works to create opportunities for young people to strengthen their capacities, express their voices, develop leadership skills, and contribute positively to their communities.

4. Our Objectives

What We Aim to Achieve

Promote education and lifelong learning opportunities for youth.

Develop leadership and life skills among young people.

Increase youth participation in community decision-making.

Promote protection, dignity, inclusion, and equal opportunities.

Support youth-led community initiatives.

Strengthen awareness of rights and responsibilities.

Encourage peaceful coexistence and community solidarity.

Promote skills development and livelihood-related opportunities.

Build partnerships with local, national, and international organizations.

Create platforms where Rohingya youth can share their ideas, concerns, and solutions.

5. Our Core Values

Principles That Guide Us

Dignity

6. Our Key Programs

Empowering Youth Across Key Areas

Education & Learning

RYDSN promotes access to educational opportunities and supports initiatives that help young people develop knowledge, confidence, and essential learning skills.

  • Educational awareness & support
  • Youth literacy initiatives
  • Digital learning & workshops
  • Mentoring and peer learning

Youth Leadership & Capacity Building

RYDSN supports young people to develop leadership, communication, teamwork, and problem-solving skills.

  • Leadership training & workshops
  • Public speaking & team building
  • Peer-to-peer learning
  • Youth-led community initiatives

Skills Development

RYDSN seeks to provide young people with practical skills that can help them become more confident and prepared for future opportunities.

  • Digital & Communication skills
  • Entrepreneurship awareness
  • Career preparation & Vocational skills
  • Financial literacy

Protection & Well-Being

RYDSN promotes awareness of safety, dignity, rights, and protection, particularly for young people facing increased vulnerabilities.

Community awareness and referral approaches that help individuals understand available protection services.

Youth Advocacy

RYDSN creates opportunities for young people to communicate their experiences, priorities, ideas, and recommendations.

Listen → Understand → Organize → Advocate → Create Change

Community & Peace Building

Encourages youth participation in community resilience, positive social participation, volunteering, and teamwork.

Promotes peaceful relationships, mutual understanding, tolerance, and inclusion within communities.
