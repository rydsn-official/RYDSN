Rohingya Youth Development Society Network (RYDSN) is a youth-led organization committed to empowering Rohingya young people and communities through education, skills development, leadership, protection, advocacy, and community-based initiatives.

Learn About RYDSN Support Our Work

Mission & Vision

Empowering youth, protecting dignity, and building resilient communities.

Our Mission

“To empower Rohingya youth through education, skills development, leadership, protection, advocacy, and meaningful community participation.”

Our Vision

“A safe, empowered, educated, and resilient Rohingya community where every young person has the opportunity to learn, lead, participate, and build a better future.”

Our Key Objectives

Strategic priorities guiding RYDSN initiatives

Promote education and lifelong learning opportunities for youth.
Develop leadership and life skills among young people.
Increase youth participation in community decision-making.
Promote protection, dignity, inclusion, and equal opportunities.
Support youth-led community initiatives and local solidarity.
Build strategic partnerships with local, national, and international partners.

Our Areas of Work

Key domains where RYDSN creates meaningful impact

Education & Learning

Educational awareness, learning support, literacy, digital learning, mentoring, and workshops.

Youth Leadership

Capacity building, public speaking, teamwork, problem-solving, and youth-led initiatives.

Skills Development

Digital skills, career preparation, entrepreneurship awareness, and financial literacy.

Protection & Well-Being

Promoting dignity, safety awareness, rights protection, and referral mechanisms for vulnerable groups.

Youth Advocacy

Providing platforms for youth voices: Listen → Understand → Organize → Advocate → Create Change.

Peace & Social Cohesion

Building bridges between communities, encouraging solidarity, tolerance, and peaceful coexistence.
